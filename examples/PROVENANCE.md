# Example Data Provenance

The files under `examples/` are small synthetic/demo fixtures created for PrivateLabBench software and evaluation-workflow testing. They are not proprietary customer data and are not copied from a restricted scientific dataset.

## License

The tabular/CSV example fixtures under `examples/` are released as **CC0-1.0** for reuse in documentation, tests, and demonstrations.

The generated Markdown reports under `examples/reports/` are derived from those synthetic fixtures and may be regenerated by the project.

## Declared CSV fixtures

The machine-readable public-release manifest at [`../audit/public_release_manifest.yaml`](../audit/public_release_manifest.yaml) enumerates every tracked `examples/**/*.csv` file. CI fails if a new CSV appears without a provenance/license declaration.

Current fixture families include:

- simple molecule/SMILES property examples;
- multi-lab synthetic molecule CSVs;
- generic prediction tables;
- multiclass probability tables;
- candidate-versus-baseline prediction examples;
- generic tabular slice examples.

SMILES strings representing simple molecules are used as test identifiers/metadata; the associated labels and prediction values are synthetic demo values.

Community benchmark packs have their own `pack.yaml` provenance declarations under `privatelabbench/data/benchmark_packs/` and are separately required to declare synthetic provenance plus CC0-1.0 licensing.

## Contribution rule

Do not add real private/restricted samples, customer data, credentials, unpublished partner data, or dataset excerpts whose license does not permit redistribution. Prefer synthetic data or a clearly documented fetch process for public datasets.
